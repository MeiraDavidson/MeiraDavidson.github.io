Calculus I

The Mathematics Foundation

Single-variable calculus — the math of how things change and pile up — earned step by step. Sequences and limits (what “getting arbitrarily close” really means), the derivative as the slope at a single point and the rules for finding it, the strange number e, the Mean Value Theorem, Taylor series (building a whole curve out of its slopes), maxima and minima, Newton's method and gradient descent (how a computer chases an answer it can't solve outright), the integral, the Fundamental Theorem that ties differentiation and integration together, and infinite series.
