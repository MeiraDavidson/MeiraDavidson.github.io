A First Taste of Algorithms

The Computing & Machine Learning Foundation

An algorithm is just a recipe a computer follows, one step at a time. This book builds that idea up from scratch — how to count the steps a method takes (and why that suddenly matters when the numbers get big), how puzzles made of dots and lines get solved, how a little randomness can actually help, and how a program can roll “downhill” to find the best answer. By the end you've met the first simple programs that learn from examples. Nothing is taken on faith; every idea is something you can run and watch happen.
